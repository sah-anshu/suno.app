Suno ("listen") helps older people, and children and adults with neurological or cognitive disabilities, hear the people and sounds around them. It works without internet, accounts or ads, and it is free and open source (GPL-3.0).

* One big Listen button, volume steps and places: Home, Park, Traffic, Night
* The patient's own hearing profile is applied to everything they hear
* Family members talk from their own phones, straight into the patient's earphones (Suno Companion)
* TV sound in the earphones, while the TV speakers stay normal for everyone else (Suno TV or a cable)
* SOS: a calm question first, then family phones are alerted, the helper is called and the position is texted
* Phone app: People with large tiles, a large keypad, simple Answer and Decline buttons, warnings for unknown callers
* Large buttons, words with every icon, colour-blind-safe colours, yellow on black, Hindi
* A helper PIN protects the settings

Everything stays on the phone. Family devices connect directly over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth, only after the patient's phone allows them, and everything is encrypted.

IMPORTANT: Suno is not a medical device or a certified hearing aid and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Hearing checks are uncalibrated estimates. SOS helps get attention but does not replace emergency services and can fail without signal or battery. Start quietly and stop if sound is too loud. This is an early test release, not yet checked on real devices.
